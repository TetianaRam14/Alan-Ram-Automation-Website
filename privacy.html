Privacy Policy / Privātuma politika

This website presents business automation services. For Business Booking Bot deployments, the client business normally determines the purposes of booking-data processing and acts as data controller; technical processing may be performed to provide and support the solution.

Data

The solution may process names, phone numbers, Telegram username/ID, selected service, booking date/time and technical request metadata.

Third-party services

Depending on the implementation, Telegram, Google Sheets/Google Workspace and a hosting provider may be used.

Rights

Requests concerning a specific booking should normally be addressed to the business with which the booking was made.
